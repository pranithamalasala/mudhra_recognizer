Mudra Library

Explore the definitions and meanings of the supported Hasta Mudras.

1. Pataka (Flag)

All fingers straight and together. Used to denote a forest, river, or beginning of a dance.

2. Tripataka (Three Parts)

Ring finger bent, others straight. Used to denote a crown, tree, or lifting a veil.

3. Shikharam (Peak)

Thumbs up gesture. Used to denote a bow, pillar, or questioning.

4. Ardhapataka (Half Flag)

Index and Middle fingers extended. Used to denote a dagger, tower, or horn.

5. Mayura (Peacock)

Ring finger touches thumb tip. Used to denote a peacock, creeper, or bird.

6. Arala (Bent)

Index finger bent. Used to denote drinking poison or a strong wind.
